About Future Spark

High school students building STEM kits for kids in Central Ohio

Our Story

Future Spark began as a service project for an English class. Students were asked to find a gap for marginalized students in the community and to bridge it.

I'm Satvik Surapaneni, a high school student in Columbus, and the gap I kept noticing was this: we ask kids what they want to be when they grow up, but a lot of them decide they are "not an engineering person" without ever building a circuit, designing a bridge, or knowing what an engineer actually does.

I didn't get interested in engineering because someone explained the definition to me. I liked making things, messing something up, figuring out what went wrong, and trying again. So a few of us started designing kits, buying the parts, and packing them on the living room floor. We deliver them to schools ourselves.

We don't expect one kit to turn every student into an engineer. We just want every kid to get the chance to try.

Who We Are

Future Spark is run by high school students in Columbus, Ohio. We design the kits, raise the money for materials, assemble everything by hand, and deliver the kits in person.

Satvik Surapaneni

Founder

Satvik is a high school student in Columbus. He started Future Spark as a class service project and now leads kit design, fundraising, and deliveries.

The Team

A group of high school students who build and deliver the kits on evenings and weekends. Our funding so far has come from door-to-door fundraising and school events.

Want to join us? Volunteer here.

Why Kits?

Elementary classrooms spend very little time on science, and the schools with the fewest resources tend to get the least. A kit is the simplest way we know to let a kid build something real with their own hands.

Every kit contains two hands-on activities, all the materials, and step-by-step instructions, so a student can complete it in class, after school, or at home without needing special equipment.

Press

Future Spark is a student-led initiative in Columbus, Ohio, that designs, hand-assembles, and delivers free hands-on STEM kits to under-resourced elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations in Central Ohio. Started by high school student Satvik Surapaneni as a class service project, the team has delivered 300 kits to three elementary schools and three community organizations at no cost to students, and is working toward 1,000 kits across Ohio.

Press
Future Spark is a student-led initiative in Columbus, Ohio, that designs, builds, and delivers free hands-on STEM kits to elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations. Founded by high school student Satvik Surapaneni as a class project, the team has delivered 300 kits at no cost to students and is working toward 1,000 across Ohio.

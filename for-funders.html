Information for Funders and Sponsors

The program, the numbers, the budget, and how to reach us

The Problem

Interest in science forms before middle school, yet K–3 classrooms average 18 minutes of science a day, and only 63% of Ohio fifth graders test proficient in science. Kids in under-resourced Central Ohio schools rarely get to build anything before deciding engineering "isn't for them."

Our Solution

High school students design and hand-assemble a two-activity STEM kit with all materials and instructions, then deliver it in person, free, through elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations. Zero cost and zero paperwork for the partner.

Scale So Far

300 kits delivered to 3 elementary schools and 3 community organizations in Central Ohio as of September 2026, funded by door-to-door fundraising and school events. Goal: 1,000 kits to 10 or more schools across Ohio.

The Ask

Materials funding. $25 makes one kit; $2,500 makes 100. Sponsorship lets us buy materials in bulk, lower the per-kit cost, and reach schools outside Columbus. Contact Satvik Surapaneni, founder, at futuresparks0@gmail.com.

Program Logic Model

InputsActivitiesOutputsOutcomes
  • Student volunteer hours for design, assembly, and delivery
  • Materials at $25 per kit
  • Sponsorships, door-to-door fundraising, school events
  • Partner sites: 3 schools, 3 community organizations
  • Design and test two activities per kit
  • Hand-assemble kits with all materials and instructions
  • Take requests from schools and organizations
  • Deliver in person, free of charge
  • Kits delivered: 300 to date, 1,000 goal
  • Sites served: 6 to date, 10+ schools goal
  • Cost per kit: $25
  • Short term: students complete a build and answer "Would you want to try engineering again?" (measured starting this school year)
  • Medium term: partner sites request kits again
  • Long term: more students from under-resourced schools choose STEM classes and activities

Budget and Funding

$25 makes one kit. That covers materials only: the activity components, the bag, and printed instructions. Every hour of design, assembly, and delivery is donated by our students, so there is no overhead and no salaries.

Funding to date: door-to-door fundraising and school events during our pilot phase. We have not received grants yet.

Why bulk matters: buying materials for 1,000 kits at once lowers the cost of every kit and lets us reach schools beyond Columbus for the first time.

Status and Accountability

Future Spark is a student-led initiative based in Columbus, Ohio. We are not yet a registered charitable organization, and we are working toward partnering with a fiscal sponsor so that grants and larger gifts can be received and receipted properly. Sponsorships received today go directly toward kit materials, and we are glad to share a summary of kits delivered and money spent with any sponsor on request.

How It Works

Three simple steps from a box of parts to a student's desk

1

We design and build the kits

Our students design each kit and assemble every one by hand. Each kit has two activities, all the materials, and step-by-step instructions.

2

Schools and organizations tell us their numbers

Elementary schools, shelters, and community groups fill out one short form and let us know how many students they serve.

3

We deliver the kits, free of charge

We bring the kits in person. Kids open the bag, build, and find out what they think. There is no cost to anyone.

What's Inside a Kit

Everything a student needs is in the bag. No extra supplies, no special tools.

  • Two hands-on activities. One of our builds is a sail car, where kids put together a wooden frame, add wheels, and attach a sail to race it.
  • All parts included. Every piece a student needs is in the bag, labeled with its contents.
  • Step-by-step instructions. Written so kids can follow along on their own or with a teacher.
  • Sealed and ready. Each kit is packed by hand and sealed before delivery.

Cost per kit: $25, materials only. Our students donate all of the labor.

Our Programs

Classroom Kit Delivery

Our main program. We deliver a full set of kits to an elementary classroom or school, one kit per student. Kids can build during class, after school, or at home.

What the school does: Tell us the number of students and the grade level, then receive the kits. That's it.

So far: 3 elementary schools in Central Ohio.

Shelters and Community Organizations

We also supply kits to shelters, after-school programs, and community centers, where kids may not be reached through a school-day class.

What the organization does: Tell us roughly how many kids you see and how often, and we size the delivery to match.

So far: 3 organizations, including a family shelter network and a community makerspace.

Common Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Kits are free to schools, organizations, families, and students.

Who builds the kits?

High school students on our team, by hand. Materials are paid for by sponsors and by our own fundraising.

Where do you deliver?

Central Ohio, including Columbus and Delaware County. Our goal is to reach schools across the state.

Do teachers need to prepare anything?

No. Every kit includes its own instructions and materials.

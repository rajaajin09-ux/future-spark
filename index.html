Free STEM Kits for Elementary Schools in Central Ohio

We are a group of high school students in Columbus who design, build, and deliver hands-on STEM kits to under-resourced elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations. Every kit is free.

What We Do

Kids often decide they are "not an engineering person" before they have ever built anything. Our kits give them a chance to try.

We Build the Kits

Our students design and hand-assemble every kit. Each one contains two hands-on activities, all the materials, and step-by-step instructions.

Schools Tell Us Their Numbers

Elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations let us know how many students they serve. That is all we need from them.

We Deliver, Free of Charge

We bring the kits in person. There is no cost to the school, the family, or the student.

What's in a Kit

Every kit comes in a sealed bag with everything a student needs to complete two hands-on activities on their own. One of our builds is a sail car: a wooden frame, wheels, and a sail that kids assemble and race.

  • Two hands-on activities
  • All parts and materials included
  • Step-by-step instructions written for kids
  • Packed by hand by our students

$25 covers the materials for one kit. Our students donate their time to design, assemble, and deliver them.

Our Story

Future Spark started as a class project. Our founder, Satvik Surapaneni, was asked in an English class to find a gap for marginalized students in the community and bridge it.

The gap he kept seeing was simple: lots of kids never get the chance to build a circuit or design a bridge, so they never find out whether they like it. We started building kits on the living room floor and delivering them to schools ourselves.

You don't know whether you like something until you try it. We make sure every kid gets to try.Satvik Surapaneni, Founder

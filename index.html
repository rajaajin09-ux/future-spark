FUTURESPARK

Future Spark is building free hands-on STEM kits so every kid gets to try engineering.

We're starting with the schools that get the least science. High school students in Columbus design each kit, buy the parts, pack every bag by hand, and deliver them in person to elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations across Central Ohio. It costs the school nothing.

Every kit holds two hands-on activities, all the materials, and step-by-step instructions a kid can follow alone. $25 covers the materials for one kit. Kids open the bag, build, and find out what they think.

Our kits are built by hand

Future Spark started as a class project. Students were asked to find a gap for marginalized kids in the community and bridge it. Founder Satvik Surapaneni kept noticing the same thing: we ask kids what they want to be, but a lot of them decide they're "not an engineering person" without ever building a circuit or designing a bridge.

Nobody gets interested in engineering from a definition. You get interested by making something, breaking it, and figuring out why. So a few of us started designing kits, buying parts, and packing them on the living room floor. We deliver them ourselves, on evenings and weekends, funded so far by door-to-door fundraising and school events.

What's inside every kit

Two activities

One of them is a sail car: a wooden frame, wheels, and a sail that kids put together and race.

All the parts

Every piece a kid needs is in the bag, labeled with its contents. Nothing to buy, no special tools.

Packed by hand

Our students assemble, label, and seal every kit themselves.

Delivered free

We bring the box to the school or organization in person. No cost, no paperwork.

Put a kit on a student's desk

A school tells us how many students it serves. We build the kits and bring them. That's the whole process.

Schools, shelters, and community organizations in Central Ohio can request kits with one short form. Sponsors cover the materials: $25 makes one kit, $250 makes a classroom set. Volunteers help us build, deliver, and write instructions.

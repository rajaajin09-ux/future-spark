FUTURESPARK

Free, hand-built STEM kits for elementary schools in Central Ohio. Built by high school students.

Future Spark is building free STEM kits so every kid gets to try engineering before deciding it isn't for them.

We're starting with the schools that get the least science. Our students design each kit, buy the parts, pack every bag by hand, and deliver them in person to elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations in Central Ohio. It costs the school nothing.

Every kit has two hands-on activities, all the materials, and step-by-step instructions written so a kid can follow them alone. One of our builds is a sail car: a wooden frame, wheels, and a sail that kids put together and race. $25 covers the materials for one kit.

Our kits are built by hand

Future Spark started as a class project. Students were asked to find a gap for marginalized kids in the community and bridge it.

Founder Satvik Surapaneni, a high school student in Columbus, kept noticing the same thing: we ask kids what they want to be, but a lot of them decide they're "not an engineering person" without ever building a circuit or designing a bridge. Nobody gets interested in engineering from a definition. You get interested by making something, breaking it, and figuring out why.

So a few students started designing kits, buying parts, and packing them on the living room floor. We deliver them ourselves, on evenings and weekends. Our funding so far has come from door-to-door fundraising and school events.

# Future Spark logo — a proposal, not a decision

1. **The mark** is a kit bag: a rounded rectangle with the red zip seal drawn across it, and a four-point spark coming out of the top-right corner. It says "kit" first and "spark" second, which is the right order for an org whose whole story is a physical bag of parts.
2. **The red** is taken from the zip seal on the bags in the team's own photos (`#C9301C`). It is the site's only accent, so the logo and the primary button share one color on purpose.
3. **Files:** `future-spark-mark.svg` (mark alone), `future-spark-logo.svg` (mark + wordmark, color), `future-spark-logo-mono.svg` (one color, ink), `future-spark-logo-reversed.svg` (white on navy), `favicon.svg` (mark on a navy rounded square, heavier strokes for 16–32 px).
4. **Wordmark:** Bricolage Grotesque Bold, tracked −1 px. The lockup SVGs reference the font by name and fall back to a bold sans if it isn't installed; before printing anything, open the SVG in a vector editor and convert the text to outlines.
5. **Minimum size:** 24 px tall for the lockup, 16 px for the favicon. Below that, use the mark alone.
6. **Clear space:** the height of the spark on all sides.
7. **Don'ts:** no gradients, no shadows, no stretching, no recoloring the seal, never place it over a busy photo.
8. **Rejected concepts** (see `screenshots/logo-concepts.png`): **B, "circuit spark"** (spark with a trace and node) reads as fintech and loses the node at 16 px; **C, "FS lettermark / spark dotting the wordmark"** works as a favicon but says nothing about kits, and the spark-in-wordmark collapses at small sizes.
9. **Scoring, out of 5:** reads as hands-on STEM 4 · works at 32 px 5 · works in one color 5 · not a template 4. Weak point: four-point sparks are common; the seal line is what makes it specific.
10. **Status:** the team should approve or reject this before it goes on anything printed. The gold spark-and-swoosh in the earlier draft site may be an existing logo; if it is official, use it and delete these files.

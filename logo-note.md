# Logo

The mark is a kit bag with its zip seal and a spark coming out of the corner. Files in `images/`: `mark.svg` (mark only, used in the header and footer), `logo.svg` (mark + wordmark), `logo-mono.svg`, `logo-white.svg`, `favicon.svg`. `hero-kit.svg` is the large illustrated version used on the home page. Colors: navy #1c2b4a, pink #e5567a, blue #2b8ad6, yellow #f5c242. The wordmark in the lockup SVGs is set in Nunito Black with an Arial fallback; convert the text to outlines before printing.

# Logo

The mark is a kit bag with its zip seal and a spark coming out of the corner. Files in `images/`: `mark.svg` (mark only, used in the header and footer), `logo.svg` (mark + wordmark), `logo-mono.svg`, `logo-white.svg`, `favicon.svg`. `hero-kit.svg` is the large illustrated version used on the home page. Colors: near-black #061a1e, blue #1f5fd6, yellow #ffbd00, light blue #bcd6ff. The wordmark in the lockup SVGs is set in Nunito Black with an Arial fallback; convert the text to outlines before printing.

About Future Spark

Future Spark is a student-led initiative in Columbus, Ohio, that designs, hand-assembles, and delivers free hands-on STEM kits to under-resourced elementary schools, shelters, and community organizations in Central Ohio. Started by high school student Satvik Surapaneni as a class service project, the team has delivered 300 kits to three elementary schools and three community organizations at no cost to students, and is working toward a goal of 1,000 kits across Ohio.

Key Facts

What
Free, hand-built STEM kits for elementary students. Two activities, all materials, and instructions in every kit.
Who
High school students in Columbus, Ohio. Founder: Satvik Surapaneni.
Where
Central Ohio, including Columbus and Delaware County.
Numbers
300 kits, 3 elementary schools, 3 community organizations, $0 to students (as of September 2026).
Goal
1,000 kits to 10 or more elementary schools across Ohio.
Cost
$25 in materials per kit. Funded by door-to-door fundraising, school events, and sponsors.
Partners
Andrew's House, Family Promise of Delaware County, Makerspace Central.
Formerly
Future Engineers (renamed September 2026).
